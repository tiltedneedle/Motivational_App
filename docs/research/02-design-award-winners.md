# Design award winners 2024–2026: concrete UI findings

Sources fetched: Apple Design Awards pages (2024/2025/2026) and press releases, Google Play Best of 2024/2025 blog, Webby 2026 press release, App Store listings (~25 apps). No official source publishes hex values; colors are as described by makers.

## Apple Design Awards 2026 — https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/
- **grug — Delight & Fun winner (Ocho, NL).** One daily "grug" in caveman grammar; entire UI is hand-scribbled; one notification at sunrise; you can draw a response; long-press any word to translate; widgets; no login, no cloud; free; 18.5 MB; 4.9★. The most relevant model for a motivational app: one idea, one voice, one moment per day.
- **Structured — Inclusivity finalist (unorderly, DE).** Single vertical timeline of rounded blocks with color + icon; Live Activity buttons with haptic feedback; distinctive check-off sound (users cite it as the dopamine hook); dyslexia font, 200% type scaling, reduced-motion mode; AI is quiet (natural-language entry, Replan), never a chat surface.
- **Moonlitt — Interaction winner (Flipping Hues, IT).** "Best-in-class Liquid Glass integration"; date/time sliders as primary navigation gesture; layer system to swap information modes; always-visible compass; Live Activities; Watch Digital Crown; OpenDyslexic/Lexend options.
- **Tide Guide — Visuals & Graphics winner (Condor Digital, US).** Full-screen charts with custom animations; "sky-matching palette" that shifts across the day; tap to swap chart views; scheduled Live Activities that start themselves.
- **The Outsiders: Athlete Tracker — Interaction finalist (Gentler Stories, SI).** Animated Training Readiness visualization as the home hero; alert states use yellow/orange, never red; Liquid Glass metric cards; reorderable sections; iOS 26 only.
- **Harvee — Social Impact finalist (Peak Labs, UK).** Watch stress as 7/28-day heat maps; outlier days highlighted; wrist haptics during breathing; tone rule: "helps you develop body wisdom" rather than health worry.
- **(Not Boring) Camera — Visuals finalist.** Fully-3D interface with dynamic lighting, stereo mechanical sounds, custom haptics, swappable "colorways". Studio philosophy: "Built like a game, runs like an app."
- Also: Detail (AI video editor, Foundation Models), Guitar Wiz (Dynamic Type, Increased Contrast, Differentiate Without Color).

## Apple Design Awards 2025 — https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/
- **CapWords — Delight & Fun winner.** Photos of real objects become sticker-style flashcards; "animations unlike anything I've ever seen."
- **Lumy — Delight & Fun finalist.** Best-documented motion system: "UI elements feel gently illuminated, a subtle echo of sunlight"; **Peek Panel** shows one line ("Moon above horizon"), fluidly expands to detail, fades back automatically; morphing Quick Access bar; time-travel slider; $7.99 one-time.
- **Evolve: Daily Self-Care Coach — Inclusivity finalist.** ADHD-focused: custom routine colors, flexible day-reset time, dedicated routine-completion animation, "Compressed Motion" accessibility option, voice affirmations.
- **Opal — Social Impact finalist.** Glow-coded feature zones (yellow Score, cyan Focus Rules, blue Timer); 18+ collectible 3D gems; one composite score.
- **Vocabulary (Monkey Taps) — Visuals finalist.** Daily-switching wallpaper themes; widget wallpaper can differ from the app.
- **iA Writer** Focus Mode dims all but the current sentence; **Train Fitness** zero-input rep detection.

## Apple Design Awards 2024
- **Bears Gratitude — Delight & Fun winner.** 100+ hand-drawn bears, pastel palette; each day is a card with an encouragement; stickers for accomplishments; explicit no-guilt for missed days. $2.99.
- **Gentler Streak — Social Impact winner.** **Activity Path**: a single flowing band at 1/10/30 days ("the steady stretches, the pauses, the returns"); "Go Gentler" scales intensity down; mascot Yorhart; 2025–26 Liquid Glass update; 25 custom icons; glassy widgets.
- **How We Feel — Social Impact finalist.** Color-coded energy × pleasantness matrix to pick an emotion word; bodily-sensation tags. Free, 4.9★ (30K).
- **Ahead: Emotions Coach — finalist.** "Duolingo for EQ": 5‑minute daily activities, blob mascot, goals decomposed into daily tasks.
- **Tiimo — Inclusivity finalist (iPhone App of the Year 2025).** Color-coded timeline with countdown timers as visual anchors against task paralysis; AI Co-Planner.
- **Meditate — Visuals finalist.** A glowing mandala blooms through seven stages as practice accumulates.
- **Rooms** (voxel rooms in a three-tab glass UI), **Crouton** (step-by-step cook mode; face-gesture page turns).

## Cross-cutting patterns winners share
1. **One thing per day, no guilt** (grug at sunrise, The Way, Bears Gratitude, Ahead).
2. **A hand-drawn character carries the emotion** — never stock illustration.
3. **One signature visualization instead of generic charts** (Activity Path, readiness animation, Opal glow score, blooming mandala, emotion matrix, stress heat map).
4. **Liquid Glass is the 2026 baseline** on tab bars, cards, toggles; progressive disclosure like Lumy's Peek Panel.
5. **Multisensory completion feedback** (Structured sound + haptics; Not Boring dials; Meditate bell-or-haptic).
6. **Semantic, restrained color** (sky-matched palette; yellow/orange not red; glow per feature).
7. **Accessibility shipped as design** (dyslexia fonts, Compressed Motion, Differentiate Without Color).
8. **Live on every surface** (widgets, Live Activities, Watch complications, Control Center).
9. **AI stays quiet** (natural-language entry, Replan, suggestions) — never a chat window as the primary UI.
10. **Privacy as a feature** (no login, on-device Health reads, "no data collected").
