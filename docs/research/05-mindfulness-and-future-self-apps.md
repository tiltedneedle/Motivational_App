# Mindfulness incumbents and "future self" apps (fetched Sept 2026)

## Insight Timer — https://insighttimer.com/
- Positioning: "#1 free meditation app"; 300K+ free tracks; MemberPlus $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr; 4.9★ (446K), Editors' Choice; 32M registered users, 16M intentions set in 2025.
- Jan 6 2026: New Year's resolution pinned on home all year, daily intention prompts, 31‑day Intention Setting Challenge, shareable graphics, and an **"AI librarian"** that reads your typed intention and recommends tracks (recommendation only, no coach).
- Loved: "seemingly limitless" free library; long-term life change stories. Complaints: rewind paywalled in free courses; streak reset 588 → 4 with slow support; post-meditation surveys intrusive ("feels like a game"); glitches after updates.
- Visual: bright, social-media-style "stories", audio-first catalog; reads as a content catalog rather than a designed experience.

## Calm — https://www.calm.com/press
- 4.8★ (2M), 180M+ downloads; $14.99/mo, $69.99–$79.99/yr, $399.99 lifetime, family $99.99/yr, 7‑day trial. Est. 3.5–4.5M paying subs (down from ~5M peak in 2022).
- No consumer AI coaching as of Jul 2026 reviews. Pivot to Calm Health (B2B clinical programs; UnitedHealthcare 13M+ members). "Just One Step" 30‑day New Year Reset (Dec 2025).
- Loved: Sleep Stories; nightly family ritual. Complaints: paid users shown "7 day trial", lost favorites; keyboard bug in gratitude journal; free content hard to find; celebrity branding heavy.
- Visual: azure gradient + mountain/lake scene carried from icon to home; player with no scrubbing.

## Headspace / Ebb — https://www.headspace.com/ebb
- 4.8★ (974K); $12.99/mo, $69.99/yr, family $99.99/yr, student $9.99/yr; 7/14‑day trials.
- **Ebb**: text or voice chat trained on motivational interviewing; reflects back, remembers, recommends content; Jumpstart prompts ("I need a pep talk"); crisis detection with escalation; "not a substitute for human care"; paid members 18+ in US/UK/CA/AU. Metrics: 64% "felt heard"; Ebb users 34% more engaged; 7M+ messages by Dec 2025. Voice mode + enhanced memory shipped Dec 8 2025. Apple Watch app (May 2026) uses HRV to time breathing nudges; "SOS mode".
- Independent review: memory "inconsistent recall", limited to light check-ins.
- Complaints: "everything was locked" (no free tier); playback controls vanish; accidental horizontal swipes; users left when founder Andy's voice was removed.
- Visual: white ground, teal/turquoise + soft green + warm yellow, geometric abstract shapes and simplified silhouettes; Ebb marketing in pink/coral with clouds.

## Lifesum — not a motivation app; "Life Score" + habit reminders; multimodal AI food logging (photo/voice/text) since Feb 2025; 4.6★ (151K); $7.49/mo, $49.99/yr. Complaints: login loops after updates, upsells, unanswered support.

## FutureMe — https://www.futureme.org/
- 2002 letter-to-future-self email service; 20M+ letters; app 2.8★ (135). Premium ~$9/yr or $119 lifetime; 2025 paywalled attachments + more ads; Dec 2025 app rebuilt as a WebView ("just a website wrapped into an app"; crashes; drafts wiped). Open lane: people still want letters to their future self and hate the current execution.

## Future-self products
- **Future You (MIT Media Lab)** — https://futureyou.media.mit.edu/ — chat with an AI simulation of yourself at 60; survey → LLM "synthetic memory" backstory; StyleGAN age-progressed photo; RCT (188 participants): reduced anxiety, reduced "lack of motivation", stronger future self-continuity; 60K+ users, 190 countries; IEEE FIE 2025, NeurIPS Creative AI 2024; free research preview.
- **HiFutureSelf** (iOS, free, 4.8★/561) — messages to future you by time, interval, or location; secret messages; minimalist.
- **FutureSelf.ai** (waitlist) — promises future-self avatar coach, cinematic "Vision Reel" videos, voice-cloned meditations; no pricing or store listing; beige/gold luxury aesthetic.
- **MyFutureSelf Daily Habit Coach** (Success AI, 4.7★/1.1K) — AI mentor, adaptive 30‑day roadmaps, AI future-self avatar, voice chat; $6.99/wk, $9.99/mo, $19.99–$35.99/yr; Aug 2026 update: "your future self greets you with today's plan and one clear next move."

## Cross-cutting takeaways
- Only Headspace (Ebb) and Insight Timer (librarian) have real AI among incumbents; Calm has none consumer-facing.
- Goal features are shallow everywhere; none does future-self envisioning with evidence-based design.
- Complaints to design against: paywalling trivial actions, streak/data loss, ads despite paying, no free tier, intrusive surveys, confusing navigation, robotic voices.
