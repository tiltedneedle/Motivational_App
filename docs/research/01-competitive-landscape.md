## G. The ten unmet needs (backed by complaints)
1. Nobody connects **goal → plan → routine → today → evidence** in one object. Griply is closest but has no AI and Apple-only; planners plan days, trackers track habits, coaches chat.
2. **Paywalled trivialities** (rewind, send a letter, 1 free tracker) generate 1-star reviews. Free tier must be genuinely usable.
3. **Streak loss = churn.** Users beg for forgiveness models (Gentler Streak, Finch, DailyHabits are loved for it).
4. **Data loss / sync bugs** are the #1 technical complaint (Streaks, Habitica, Calm favorites, ThinkUp lifetime lockout). Local-first with reliable sync is a differentiator.
5. **Generic AI**: "underwhelming" (Todoist), "weird" (Motion), "inconsistent memory" (Ebb). Users want AI that produces plans and remembers, not chat for its own sake.
6. **"Emotionless robotic voice"** (Perfectly Happy) — voice quality matters when the product is emotional.
7. **Future-self is proven but unbuilt**: MIT RCT effects, FutureMe's 20M letters vs its 2.8‑star app, MyFutureSelf's generic execution.
8. **Nag fatigue**: subscription pop-ups, intrusive surveys (Insight Timer), "fake and obnoxious" upsells (Reflectly).
9. **Widget regressions** cause rage — widgets are a core surface, not a nice-to-have.
10. **Clichéd content**: quotes and affirmations feel canned; personalization from the user's own words is the lever (ThinkUp's own-voice recordings are its most praised feature).

## H. What "vibe-coded" looks like in this category (avoid)
Product Hunt now has apps literally titled "vibe coded habit tracker" (Habi). Common tells: indigo/violet gradient buttons, Inter everywhere, uniform rounded-2xl cards with soft shadows, emoji as icons (Routinery's 800 emoji), Lottie confetti on every completion, GitHub-style green heatmap, generic "Sparkles" AI icon, chat-bubble AI as the primary surface, stock 3D blob illustrations, percent rings everywhere, purple "I Am"-style theme packs.
Distinctive incumbents and why: Structured (proportional timeline, one icon system), Opal (glow semantics + 3D gems), Gentler Streak (Activity Path + mascot), grug (scribbles, one sunrise notification), Tide Guide (sky-matched palette), Not Boring (3D + haptics + sound), The Way (single linear path, no library), Finch (character whose energy is the consequence of your check-in).
